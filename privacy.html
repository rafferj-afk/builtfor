BUILTFOR — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Who we are

BUILTFOR is operated by Joe Rafferty (pkqdg7v24b@privaterelay.appleid.com).

What we collect

The answers you give in the quiz (including height, weight, body-fat estimate, sex, age and country), your quiz results, and — only if you choose to unlock the full deck — your email address.

Why

To show and email you your results, and to improve the matching model in aggregate.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the email form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us to request deletion.

Storage

Data is stored using Supabase (EU region). We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Retention

We keep responses until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or erasure of your data, and you can object to processing. Email pkqdg7v24b@privaterelay.appleid.com. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
